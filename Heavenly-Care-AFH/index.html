Frequently Asked Questions

What levels of care do you provide?
We provide levels 1 through 3 of care, ranging from minimal assistance with daily activities to full assistance with mobility, feeding, and incontinence care. We also specialize in Dementia and mental health support.
What are the visiting hours?
We encourage families to visit! Our typical visiting hours are 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily. However, we are flexible and can accommodate special requests to ensure you stay connected with your loved one.
Is the staff available 24/7?
Yes, we have awake staff available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is always a trained caregiver on-site to assist with emergencies, toileting, or simply to provide comfort during the night.
How are medications managed?
We utilize a strict medication delegation and administration protocol overseen by a registered nurse. All medications are stored securely and dispensed at prescribed times, with detailed logging.
Do you accept Medicaid?
We primarily accept private pay, but we may have limited spots for Medicaid or Long Term Care Insurance. Please contact us directly to discuss current availability and financial options.
