Licensed & Certified

State-licensed Adult Family Home complying with all Washington regulations for safety and care.

The "Whole Person" Approach

We believe care goes beyond medication. It encompasses emotional well-being, social connection, and physical dignity.

Physical Wellness

Our team monitors vital signs, skin integrity, and mobility daily. We coordinate directly with physical therapists and doctors to maintain strength and prevent decline, ensuring the highest physical standard of living.

Emotional Support

Mental health is a priority. Through validation therapy, active listening, and a calm environment, we reduce anxiety and depression. We treat our residents with the patience and love of family members.

Social Connection

Isolation is the enemy of aging. We foster a community atmosphere where meals are shared, birthdays are celebrated, and interaction is encouraged, while respecting the need for privacy.

Comprehensive Assistance

Core Services

All-inclusive care designed to eliminate the stress of daily chores and medical logistics.

Hygiene & Grooming

Dignified assistance with bathing, dressing, toileting, and personal grooming. We help residents look and feel their best every day.

Medication Management

Nurse-delegated administration, pharmacy coordination, and precise logging to ensure the right dose at the right time, every time.

Nutritional Dining

Three home-cooked meals daily plus snacks. Diets tailored to medical needs (diabetic, low sodium, pureed) without sacrificing flavor.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Daily cleaning, fresh linens, and personal laundry services. We maintain a spotless, sanitary, and clutter-free environment.

Mobility Assistance

Safe transfers (one or two-person assist), wheelchair maneuvering, and fall prevention monitoring to ensure safe movement throughout the home.

Respite & Hospice

Flexible options for short-term stays to relieve family caregivers, as well as compassionate end-of-life care coordinated with hospice agencies.

High Acuity Expertise

We are equipped to handle complex medical conditions that many standard facilities cannot. Our staff undergoes specialized training to manage these needs safely in a home setting.

Discuss specific needs

Dementia & Alzheimer's

Safety measures for wandering, memory-enhancing activities, and staff trained in de-escalation and validation techniques.

Diabetes Management

Blood glucose monitoring, insulin administration, and strict dietary planning to maintain stable levels.

Stroke Recovery

Assistance with paralysis, aphasia, and rehabilitation exercises prescribed by therapists to regain independence.

Incontinence Care

Discreet, dignified management of bowel and bladder incontinence, including catheter care (Foley/Suprapubic).

Oxygen & Respiratory

Management of O2 concentrators, nebulizers, and CPAP/BiPAP machines for sleep apnea and COPD support.

Tube Feeding (PEG)

Specialized care for residents requiring enteral nutrition via feeding tubes, ensuring proper hygiene and caloric intake.
