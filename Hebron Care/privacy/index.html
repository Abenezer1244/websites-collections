Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Hebron Care Adult Family Home LLC #1 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website or contact us.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form or reach us by phone or email, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any message or inquiry you send. We do not collect sensitive personal data through this website unless you voluntarily provide it.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiries, schedule tours, and provide information about our care services. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the website (for example, our contact-form processor) under strict confidentiality.

Retention and security

We keep contact and inquiry information only as long as needed to fulfill your request and for reasonable business and legal purposes. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to delete it. To do so, contact us at hebroncareafhllc@gmail.com or call (360) 843-7364.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our site or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hebroncareafhllc@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
