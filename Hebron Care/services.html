Our Care Approach

We believe that true care extends far beyond physical health. Our methodology is rooted in a holistic understanding of each individual, combining clinical precision with genuine emotional support.

Highly Personalized

Before a resident moves in, we conduct an exhaustive assessment to understand not just their medical requirements, but their life history, dietary preferences, and daily rhythms. Every care plan is uniquely bespoke.

Proactive Wellness

We don't just react to health changes; we anticipate them. Through continuous monitoring and close coordination with primary care physicians, we actively manage health to prevent hospital readmissions.

Family Integration

When a resident joins us, their family becomes our family. We maintain transparent, regular communication channels with relatives, ensuring you are always informed, involved, and welcomed in our home.

Core Services

Our foundational services are designed to remove the friction from daily life, allowing our residents to focus on what matters most: enjoying their days in comfort and dignity.

Discuss care options

Daily Living Support

Discreet, respectful assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) including bathing, dressing, grooming, and personal hygiene, tailored to the resident's desired level of independence.

Medication Management

Flawless administration of all medications. We handle prescription refills, coordinate with pharmacies, and ensure the right dosages are given exactly on schedule to maintain optimal health.

Nutritional Dining

Three delicious, home-cooked meals daily, plus healthy snacks. We accommodate all dietary restrictions (diabetic, low-sodium, pureed) without ever compromising on flavor or presentation.

Mobility & Transfers

Expert assistance with walking, wheelchair navigation, and safe transferring from bed to chair. Our facility is fully equipped to prevent falls and support residents with varying physical limitations.

Specialized Medical Care

Some conditions require a higher tier of vigilance and specialized training. Our team is fully certified to manage complex medical situations with grace and expertise.

Dementia & Memory Care

We provide a secure, easily navigable environment for residents experiencing Alzheimer's and other forms of dementia. Our caregivers are trained in de-escalation, redirection, and validation therapy to ensure residents feel safe, understood, and calm.

Hospice & Palliative Support

In partnership with local hospice agencies, we offer profound end-of-life care focusing on pain management and comfort. We provide a peaceful, loving sanctuary for residents and their families during life's most delicate transitions.

Post-Stroke Rehabilitation

Recovery from a stroke requires patience and consistency. We coordinate closely with visiting physical, occupational, and speech therapists, integrating their prescribed exercises into our daily care routines to optimize recovery.

Diabetic & Incontinence Care

From meticulous blood sugar monitoring and insulin administration to discreet, dignified incontinence management, we handle chronic conditions efficiently to prevent complications and maintain the resident's comfort and pride.

Facility Amenities

A clinical setting can never feel like home. We designed Hebron Care to be a sanctuary of comfort, engagement, and security.

Private & Semi-Private Rooms

Spacious, beautifully furnished bedrooms bathed in natural light. Residents are encouraged to bring personal belongings to make the space truly their own.

Accessible Gardens

Secure, professionally landscaped outdoor areas with paved walking paths, raised garden beds, and shaded patios perfect for afternoon tea or family visits.

Advanced Security Systems

Peace of mind with 24/7 monitored alarms, delayed-egress doors to prevent wandering, and emergency call bell systems located in every bedroom and bathroom.

Entertainment & Wi-Fi

High-speed internet throughout the home allows for easy video calls with family. Common areas feature large screens for movie nights and current events.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Comprehensive domestic care including daily room tidying, deep cleaning, and full laundry services for personal clothing and fresh linens provided regularly.

Transport Coordination

We arrange and coordinate safe, reliable transportation for medical appointments, ensuring residents get to their doctors seamlessly and on time.
