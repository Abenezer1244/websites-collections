Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions families have when considering our home.

What is the staff-to-resident ratio?
We maintain a very low staff-to-resident ratio, typically 1 caregiver to 3 or 4 residents. This ensures that every individual receives prompt, attentive, and highly personalized care throughout the day and night.
Are family visits allowed at any time?
Absolutely. This is your loved one's home, and we encourage family visits. While we ask visitors to be respectful of quiet hours and meal times, you are welcome to visit, share a meal, or spend time together in our common areas or their private room.
How do you handle medical emergencies?
Our staff is trained in First Aid and CPR, and we have protocols in place for any medical situation. In the event of an emergency, we immediately contact 911, followed by the resident's designated primary contacts and their physician.
Do you accommodate special diets?
Yes. We understand that nutrition is vital for seniors. All meals are home-cooked, and we easily accommodate diabetic, low-sodium, pureed, and other prescribed or preferred diets.
What daily activities are provided?
Activities are tailored to the interests and abilities of our current residents. They typically include gentle morning exercises, arts and crafts, music therapy, movie afternoons, gardening in our secured patio, and structured cognitive games.
