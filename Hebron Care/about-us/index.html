About Us

Redefining care through
compassion & family.

We started Hebron Care with a simple, unwavering belief: every senior deserves to age with dignity in a place that truly feels like home.

Schedule a Tour Learn about our mission

Our People, our strength.

At Hebron Care, our caregivers are more than staff. They are trained professionals who treat every resident like family. With backgrounds in geriatric nursing, dementia care, and rehabilitative support, our team brings both skill and heart to everything they do.

Every member of our team holds certifications in First Aid, CPR, and specialized senior care. Many have over a decade of hands-on experience working in adult family homes across Washington State. We invest in continuous training so that our residents receive the most current, compassionate, and effective care possible.

We welcome you to visit, meet our caregivers in person, and see firsthand the warmth and professionalism that define Hebron Care.

Our Story

The milestones that shaped Hebron Care into the sanctuary it is today for families in Everett and throughout Snohomish County.

Serving families in Everett and Snohomish County since we opened our doors.

01 · The Foundation

Recognizing the Need

After years of working in large, institutional healthcare settings, our founders witnessed how easily residents could feel overlooked. The spark for Hebron Care was ignited by a passionate desire to create a true, intimate home rather than just another facility.

02 · The Launch

Opening Our Doors

Hebron Care officially opened its doors, welcoming our first residents. We established our core team of highly trained, empathetic caregivers, many of whom are still with us today. It was the moment our philosophy of care was put into beautiful practice.

03 · Present Day

Growing Our Family

Today, we are a trusted community staple. While our methods and medical training continuously evolve with modern best practices, our core promise remains identical to day one: delivering deeply personalized, compassionate care to every single person who calls Hebron Care home.

What we believe.

To provide an unwavering standard of care in a deeply personal, residential setting. We strive to enrich the daily lives of our residents by offering tailored support that honors their unique histories, preferences, and individual needs.

We intentionally limit our capacity to ensure that nobody ever feels like just a number. At Hebron Care, every resident receives the focused attention, home-cooked nutrition, and genuine companionship they deserve.

We envision a future where adult family homes are universally recognized as the gold standard for senior care, a future where warmth, empathy, and professional medical excellence blend seamlessly under one roof.

Hebron Care aims to continually be a beacon of trust for families in our community, redefining the aging process by focusing on joy, dignity, and a profound sense of belonging.
