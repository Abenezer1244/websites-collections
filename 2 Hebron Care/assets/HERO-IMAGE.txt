HERO IMAGE (Home page)
======================
Place your real photo here for the home page hero.

Filename: hero-home.jpg
(Also OK: hero-home.png or hero-home.webp — then update the <img src="..."> in index.html to match.)

Recommendations:
- Common area of your home (living room, dining, or welcoming space)
- Daylight, natural lighting
- Residents and/or staff only if you have consent
- Size: 1200×800px or larger (e.g. 1200×900, 1600×1200) for best quality

Until you add hero-home.jpg, the site will show a fallback image. Replace the file with your photo when ready.
