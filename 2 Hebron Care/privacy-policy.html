Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Hebron Care Adult Family Home LLC #1 (“we,” “us,” “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form (e.g., Request a Tour or Contact Us), we may collect your name, email, phone number, and any message you provide. We use this only to respond to your inquiry and, with your consent, to follow up about care options.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your requests, schedule tours, and communicate about our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Form Submissions

Form submissions may be processed by third-party services (e.g., Formspree) that handle delivery to our email. Please review their privacy policies for how they process data.

Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use minimal cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality. We do not use advertising or invasive tracking.

Data Retention & Security

We retain contact and inquiry data only as long as needed to fulfill your request and for legitimate business purposes. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your Rights

You may ask us what data we hold about you and request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law. Contact us at hebroncareafhllc@gmail.com or (425) 225-5424.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
