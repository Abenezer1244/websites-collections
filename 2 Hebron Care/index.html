Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What level of care do you provide?

We provide comprehensive adult family home care, which includes assisted living, memory care, medication management, and support with all activities of daily living (ADLs). Our skilled staff is available 24/7 to address individual needs.

Are meals tailored to dietary needs?

Yes, nutrition is a priority. We prepare three delicious, home-cooked meals daily, along with healthy snacks. All meals can be customized to accommodate specific dietary restrictions, medical requirements, or personal preferences.

What are the visiting hours for family?

We encourage strong family connections. We have an open-door policy for families, allowing visits at any reasonable time that is comfortable for the resident. This is their home, and family is always welcome.

How do you handle medical emergencies?

Our staff is rigorously trained in emergency protocols, CPR, and First Aid. In the event of an emergency, we immediately contact emergency services (911), notify the family, and coordinate with the resident's primary care physician.

What is the transition process like?

We strive to make the transition as smooth as possible. It begins with a comprehensive assessment by our registered nurse to understand the clinical and personal needs. From there, we formulate a care plan and assist the family with moving logistics to ensure immediate comfort.

How many residents do you care for?

As a licensed Washington State Adult Family Home, we maintain a small, intimate setting with a low resident-to-staff ratio. This allows us to give each resident attentive, personalized care and a true family atmosphere. Please contact us for current capacity and availability.

Can my loved one bring personal furniture?

Yes. We encourage residents to bring familiar items—such as a favorite chair, photos, bedding, or a small dresser—to make their room feel like home. We work with you to ensure furniture and belongings fit safely in the space and support their comfort and independence.

Do you accept residents with dementia behaviors or wandering?

Yes. We provide specialized memory care and are experienced in supporting residents with Alzheimer's, dementia, and related behaviors including wandering. Our home is secure and staffed 24/7, with structured routines and a calm environment designed to reduce confusion and promote safety and dignity. We assess each individual to ensure we can meet their needs.
