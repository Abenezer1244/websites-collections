Questions

Common Inquiries

What levels of care do you provide?
We offer a comprehensive range of care, from assisted living for those needing minimal help, to specialized memory care and high-acuity medical management. Our assessment process ensures a tailored plan for every resident.
What are the visiting hours?
We encourage family involvement. While we have flexible visiting hours between 9 AM and 8 PM daily, we can accommodate special requests to ensure you stay connected with your loved ones.
Is the facility staffed 24/7?
Yes, our home is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week by awake caregivers. We also have a registered nurse on call around the clock to address medical concerns immediately.
How do you handle dietary restrictions?
Our culinary team prepares fresh, home-cooked meals daily. We cater to all dietary needs including diabetic, low-sodium, gluten-free, and texture-modified diets, ensuring nutrition is both healthy and enjoyable.
What is the admission process?
We start with a tour and a complimentary needs assessment. Once we determine we are the right fit, we coordinate with physicians for medical orders and assist with the transition logistics to make the move seamless.
