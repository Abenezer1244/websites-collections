Who We Are

More than a facility,
we are family.

Bonbica Serenity was founded on a simple yet powerful premise: that senior care should not just be about medical oversight, but about preserving the joy, dignity, and stories of every individual.

6:1 Resident Ratio
15+ Years Experience
Our Mission

To Honor Life's Journey.

Our mission is to provide personalized, high-quality care in a setting that feels safe, familiar, and loving. We strive every day to ensure our residents feel valued, heard, and respected, maintaining their independence and dignity through compassionate support.

Our Vision

Redefining Senior Living.

We envision a world where aging is celebrated. Bonbica Serenity aims to set the gold standard for Adult Family Homes in Washington, creating a community where families have total peace of mind and residents find new purpose and joy in their golden years.
