Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our home and care levels.

What levels of care do you provide?
We provide a wide range of care levels, from assisted living for those needing minor help with daily activities to specialized dementia care and hospice support. Our staff is trained to adapt to changing medical needs.
What are the visiting hours?
We encourage family involvement! While we have open visiting hours generally between 9 AM and 8 PM, we are flexible and can accommodate visits outside these times to ensure residents stay connected with loved ones.
Is the facility staffed 24/7?
Yes, absolutely. We have awake staff 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is always a qualified caregiver or nurse on-site to assist with any needs or emergencies that may arise during the night.
Are meals customizable for dietary needs?
Nutrition is a key pillar of our care. All meals are home-cooked, and we strictly adhere to doctor-prescribed diets including diabetic, low-sodium, puréed, and renal diets.
Do you handle medication management?
Yes. We provide full medication administration and management. We work directly with pharmacies to ensure prescriptions are refilled and administered correctly and on time, every time.
