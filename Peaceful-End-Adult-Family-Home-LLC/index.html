Common Questions

What is an Adult Family Home?
An Adult Family Home (AFH) is a residential home licensed to provide personal care and room and board for up to 6 adults. Unlike large nursing facilities, we offer a cozy, family-like environment with a high staff-to-resident ratio, ensuring personalized attention 24 hours a day.
What levels of care do you provide?
We are licensed to provide care ranging from light assistance with daily living to heavy care for dementia, bed-bound residents, and hospice support. We conduct an initial assessment to ensure we can safely meet your loved one's specific medical and physical needs.
What are the visiting hours?
Peaceful End AFH is their home, so we have an open-door policy for family and friends. While we generally encourage visits between 9 AM and 7 PM to respect the sleep schedules of other residents, we are flexible to accommodate family needs.
Do you handle medical appointments?
We coordinate transportation and can accompany residents to appointments if family is unavailable (additional fees may apply). We also have visiting doctors, podiatrists, and nurses who come directly to the home for routine check-ups.
How is the cost determined?
Fees are based on the level of care required and the room chosen (private vs. semi-private). We accept private pay and select long-term care insurance policies. Please contact us for a free assessment and a transparent quote.
